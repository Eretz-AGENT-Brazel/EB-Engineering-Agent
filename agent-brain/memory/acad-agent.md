## ⚡⚡ THE BIGGEST LESSON SO FAR (2026-07-31) — BUILD THE DETAIL ONCE, THEN REPLICATE
Amir, after the exam-5 attempt ran far over its 10-minute limit: *"If you noticed, when I gave you the lessons — **I modelled once and then replicated. That is the whole principle.** You produce one detail that repeats, and then simply copy and paste it wherever it is needed, and rotate it or mirror it. It is that simple and that accessible — and you go around in circles writing scripts for really simple operations."* He also asked, plainly, *"why are you not working inside the software? why are you not using its tools? you are already in it."*
- **The arithmetic:** 20 columns each with a base plate + 3 wall plates + holes + anchors = **~500 atomic operations my way** vs **one detail (~30) + 19 copies/rotates/mirrors ≈ 70 his way.** Seven times the work for an identical model, and every extra op is another chance to misplace a coordinate.
- **I had the evidence three times and never adopted the behaviour:** lesson 4 (one rib → `PS_COPY`×7 + `MIRROR`×2 = 8), lesson 5 (`PS_COPY` was the **busiest command, ×28**), lesson 5 again (*"I made an ordinary plate and copied the anchor bolts onto it"*). I logged each as a nice touch instead of as **the method**.
- **Root cause in me:** my channel sends one command and reads one answer, and ProSteel's replication tools are **dialogs** (selection, base point, array, count). Unable to answer a dialog, I habitually decomposed everything into atomic dialog-free calls — and never questioned it. **That habit deformed my conception of the work, not just the implementation.**
- **The tool was already mine.** `clonemodel` (built in lesson 2) = `DeepCloneObjects` + `TransformBy` copies objects **with holes, connections, layers, anchors, groups intact**. I used it to duplicate a whole model and never thought of it as *replicate a detail*. Swapping `Matrix3d.Displacement` for `Matrix3d.Rotation`/mirror is a one-line change → full copy/paste/rotate/mirror. **Nothing was missing but the method.**
- **THE RULE:** before creating the *second* instance of anything, stop and replicate the first. Find the repeating unit → build it once, correctly, verify it hard → replicate to every location → verify copies against the original. **Never emit a loop of N identical creation commands** (>3 identical calls ⇒ use replication). When the tool is a dialog, find the scriptable equivalent (`clonemodel`, `_ARRAYRECT`, native `_COPY`) or **ask Amir** — he does it in seconds.
